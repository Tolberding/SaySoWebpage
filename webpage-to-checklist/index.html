Online instructions are easier to read than to follow

The information may be good even when the page is awkward during active work.

How-to pages often mix the instructions you need with introductions, ads, navigation, related links, and long explanations. That structure can be useful while learning, but it becomes cumbersome once you start the work. You scroll to find the next action, the page reloads, or you lose your place after switching apps.

SaySo helps turn the actionable parts of a webpage into a focused checklist on iPhone. Share the page once, review the organized tasks, and use the resulting list while you cook, repair, assemble, clean, plan, or create.

Separate learning from doing

The source page remains valuable for background and visual detail. The checklist serves a different purpose: it gives you the sequence of actions.

Make long guides easier to resume

Multi-step projects rarely happen without interruption. A checklist shows what is complete and what comes next, so you can return without reconstructing your position.
