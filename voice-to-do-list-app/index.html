A faster path from thinking to doing

Useful plans rarely arrive as perfectly formatted checklists.

You may know everything that needs to happen, but typing each task and subtask can turn a quick thought into a chore. SaySo gives you a voice-first way to capture that plan. Speak in your own words, include the details that matter, and let the app shape them into a to-do list you can actually use.

That makes voice capture practical for errands, household routines, project setup, repairs, meal prep, packing, and any moment when your thoughts move faster than your thumbs. Instead of recording a voice memo that you must replay later, you get a structured list designed for action.

More useful than a single reminder

Siri and reminder apps are helpful when the task is short. A multi-step plan needs more structure. SaySo is built for ideas such as cleaning the garage, preparing for a trip, organizing a room, or working through a weekend project. You can explain the whole situation and keep the context that a one-line reminder would lose.

Ready when your hands are busy

Creating the list is only half the job. SaySo also helps you move through it while cooking, building, fixing, or creating. Clear steps make it easier to resume after an interruption and keep the next action visible without returning to a long note.

Try saying

  • “Make a packing list for a three-day work trip.”
  • “Turn my garage cleanup plan into steps.”
  • “Organize these errands into a checklist.”
  • “Break this weekend project into tasks.”

Voice to-do list FAQ

Can I create a to-do list by speaking?

Yes. SaySo turns a naturally spoken plan into organized tasks and steps on iPhone.

How is it different from a voice memo?

A voice memo saves a recording. SaySo creates an actionable list you can review and follow.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use SaySo without an account, and tasks are stored locally by default.
