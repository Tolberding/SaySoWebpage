The recipe should support the cooking

Long pages and tiny screens are a poor fit for floury fingers and hot pans.

Online recipes are made to be read, but cooking is active. You measure, mix, chop, check heat, wash your hands, and move around the kitchen. Every trip back to a long recipe page creates another chance to lose your place or touch the phone with messy hands.

SaySo turns the useful parts of a recipe into a practical cooking list. Share the webpage, talk through your own meal plan, or start from a cooking video. The result is a set of steps designed to help you work, not another page to scroll.

Keep prep and cooking in the right order

A good cooking checklist surfaces prep before the moment it becomes urgent. It helps separate ingredients, setup, cook times, and finishing steps so you can see what comes next. This is especially useful for recipes with several components or meals where multiple dishes need attention.

Return to the exact next action

Kitchen interruptions are normal. A timer rings, someone asks a question, or another pan needs attention. Guided tasks give you a clear place to return. Instead of searching the original page, you can continue from the current step and stay focused on the food.
