Stop hunting through the timeline for the next step

Video is excellent at showing a technique. It is less convenient as a working checklist.

A good YouTube tutorial can teach you how to install a shelf, repair an appliance, cook a new dish, complete a workout, or make something from scratch. The friction begins when it is time to follow along. You pause, put the phone down, complete one action, then scrub backward because the creator moved on before you were ready.

SaySo turns the tutorial into an organized checklist. You can scan the overall process before starting, see what tools or ingredients may be needed, and return to a clear next action after an interruption. The original video still matters for close-up details; the checklist makes the full workflow easier to manage.

Prepare before pressing play

A checklist gives you a chance to notice materials, prerequisites, and setup steps early. That can prevent the mid-project discovery that you are missing a tool or ingredient.

Use tutorials for active projects

SaySo is particularly useful when both hands are occupied. DIY, cooking, assembly, crafts, and workouts all make repeated phone handling awkward.
